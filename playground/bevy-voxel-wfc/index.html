Bevy 3D Voxel WFC Diorama

Loading Bevy 3D Voxel WFC WebGL2 module…

Controls: Left-Drag orbit diorama · Scroll zoom · Q/E/W/S or Arrows orbit & tilt · T toggle turntable spin · R random seed · Space pause/resume animated collapse · F finish solve instantly.

Generates a deterministic 20×20×20 cutaway voxel diorama in three passes: Pass 1 runs 15-state u16 bitmask WFC with AC-3 propagation so land never floats, sea settles flat, and steep drops become cliff rock; Pass 2 runs Barnes Priority-Flood to fill enclosed highland bowls into elevated alpine ponds; Pass 3 grows complete directional-leaf trees and meadow shrubs strictly on arable grass.

Note: WebGL2 is required to run this example. If it fails to start, check that Hardware Acceleration is enabled in your browser settings.
